My Journey

A timeline of my education, experiences, challenges and growth.

School Life

I was an average student academically, somewhat shy in nature, but sincere towards my studies. I had friends and enjoyed school life while trying to do my best with the understanding I had at that time.

Class 10

Completed Class 10 from MP Board with 88.2%. This was one of the strongest academic performances of my school life.

Class 12

Completed Class 12 from MP Board with 83.4%. Although the result was decent, I felt that I could have performed better.

Missed Opportunities

I was unable to prepare properly for JEE and missed the opportunity to appear seriously in that direction. Later, I also missed an ASI examination opportunity because I checked the admit card too late.

These experiences taught me the importance of planning, consistency and staying aware of opportunities.

College Life

Currently pursuing B.Sc. Computer Science at Holkar Science College, Indore. I have also completed DCA from Makhanlal Chaturvedi University and cleared CPCT.

Moving from Dewas to Simrol

To save travel time and focus more on studies, I shifted from Dewas to Simrol during my preparation phase. This change helped me reduce daily travel and create more time for learning.

Present Phase

Still trying and in between the journey and let see where i will be reach...
