Setbacks & Lessons

Failures, challenges and the lessons they taught me.

Why This Page Exists

This website is not only about achievements. It is also about the mistakes, failures and challenges that shaped me.

I believe setbacks are an important part of growth, and ignoring them would make this website incomplete.

Academic Disappointments

Although I scored well in school, I felt that I could have performed much better in Class 12 if I had managed my preparation more effectively.

JEE Preparation

One of my biggest academic disappointments was that I was unable to prepare seriously for JEE. I missed an opportunity that many students use to enter engineering institutions.

This experience taught me the importance of planning, consistency and long-term focus.

Missing the ASI Examination

In 2025, I missed an ASI examination opportunity. When I checked the admit card information, I realized that the examination had already been conducted.

This taught me the importance of staying organized, tracking important dates and being more responsible.

Personal Challenges

  • Overthinking
  • Negative self-talk
  • Procrastination
  • Lack of consistency
  • Difficulty balancing studies and preparation
  • Fear of making the wrong career choice
  • Fear of disappointing family

Current Struggles

My current challenge is not a single exam or event. It is developing discipline, consistency and better time management in daily life.

I am still working on these areas and consider them ongoing goals rather than completed achievements.

What I Learned

  • Opportunities can be lost if not tracked carefully.
  • Consistency matters more than motivation.
  • Failure is not permanent.
  • Every mistake contains a lesson.
  • Growth takes time.
  • Improvement is often slow but meaningful.

Moving Forward

I do not define myself by my failures. I acknowledge them, learn from them and continue moving forward.

My goal is not to become perfect, but to become better than I was yesterday.
